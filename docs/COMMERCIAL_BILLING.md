# Commercial invoice and offline payment acceptance

Apply migration-003-billing.sql after the authority rotation and recovery migrations. All non-evaluation new issuance and terms renewal require an independently settled invoice for the exact contractual terms (customer, deployment, edition, nodes, sites, expiry and features). Key rotation does not change the terms fingerprint. An unchanged contract can cover all paid seats, not one invoice per activation. Existing commercial subscriptions must have an approved settlement record before any new issuance; old licenses remain valid until their signed expiry or explicit revocation. Revocation and key rotation never depend on payment. Historical committed jobs remain idempotent.

Requester creates pending invoice in minor currency units for current subscription or a specific pending/approved renewal job. Independently administered reviewer portal verifies agreed pricing and externally cleared payment, confirms exact amount and unique payment reference, or voids unpaid invoice. A paid invoice is immutable; no role can edit the amount, reuse a payment reference or delete invoices. This is manual payment attestation, not independent bank/provider verification, tax-compliant invoice generation or automatic collection/refunds. The reviewer must verify pricing against an external executed agreement. No card data, bank credentials, real customer data or live payment integration in Git. Refunds/disputes must be handled externally and any license withdrawal through approved revocation. Evaluation licenses are intentionally not billed; evaluation policy restrictions need separate acceptance. The issuer fails closed without the billing migration. Recovery checkpoints now include invoices; older checkpoint schema must not be treated as matching the expanded ledger. Recreate the trusted checkpoint after controlled migration, independently verified while mutations are fenced.


Expiry values are canonical integer Unix UTC seconds (floor, never PostgreSQL numeric rounding). Portal-created subscriptions already use exact seconds. Before migrating any manually provisioned fractional-second subscriptions, fence issuance and reconcile existing invoice fingerprints and previously issued trial claims against their approved exact terms. Never silently rewrite paid invoices or evaluation claims. New integration acceptance deliberately exercises fractional timestamps.
